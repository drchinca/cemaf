# CEMAF Philosophy

CEMAF is an **open startup** building AI infrastructure in public. Here's how we work.

## Our 10 Principles

### 1. Community First
We serve developers building AI agents. Success = value we create for users, not value we capture.

### 2. Mission Alignment
Your personal growth + CEMAF's growth should align. If they don't, speak up - we'll adjust.

### 3. Ask for Help
Working in silos is slow. Post questions publicly, share work-in-progress, admit when you're stuck. Asking for help is strength.

### 4. Open Collaboration
Anyone aligned with the mission can contribute. Voice grows through participation, not titles. Show up as you are.

### 5. Dynamic Roles
Roles based on what's needed now, not who held them before. Leadership = owning outcomes + making room for others.

### 6. Build What Matters
Focus on real infrastructure problems (token budgets, deterministic replay, provenance tracking). Test fast, kill what doesn't work.

### 7. Value Impact
Outcomes > effort. Did something exist today that helps CEMAF because of what you did?

### 8. Be Where Developers Are
Meet users where they work: Discord servers, Reddit, HN, Twitter. Don't force them to come to us.

### 9. Learn and Adapt
Hold our work lightly, mission tightly. When we learn something new, pivot without ego. Document what changed and why.

### 10. Make Space for Growth
Contributing should teach you. Pair with mentors, rotate responsibilities, celebrate learning.

---

## Our Values

**Integrity:** Do what we say. No dark patterns, no bait-and-switch.

**Community-Made:** Built WITH users, not FOR them.

**Earned Trust:** Through consistent contributions, not credentials.

**Purpose Over Noise:** Focus on mission, avoid feature bloat.

**Work That Fuels Growth:** Contribution = learning.

**Pragmatism:** Principles guide us, but we adapt to reality.

**Human, Not Polished:** Share works-in-progress and mistakes.

**Open by Default:** Public discussions, transparent metrics.

**Humility:** Best idea wins, regardless of source.

**Build What's Missing:** Solve problems others ignore.

---

## How This Shows Up

**Code Reviews:** Explain why, not just what. Merge based on quality, not seniority.

**Community Support:** Turn confusion into docs. Welcome "dumb questions."

**Decisions:** Document in `docs/decisions/`. Invite community input.

**Roadmap:** Community votes on features. Share what we're building and why.

**Metrics:** Weekly updates with learnings (wins AND mistakes).
