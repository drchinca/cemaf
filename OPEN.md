## Philosophy

We follow 10 principles (see [full philosophy](docs/philosophy.md)):

1. **Community First** - Serve developers, not revenue
2. **Mission Alignment** - Your growth + project growth
3. **Ask for Help** - No silos, collaborate openly
4. **Open Collaboration** - Anyone can contribute
5. **Dynamic Roles** - Earn trust through participation
6. **Build What Matters** - Real problems > cool features
7. **Value Impact** - Results > effort
8. **Be Where Developers Are** - Meet users in their communities
9. **Learn and Adapt** - Pivot without ego
10. **Make Space for Growth** - Contributing should teach you
